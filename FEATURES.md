# KSP DAPPA — Feature Catalog

**1,138 catalogued entries** — 808 from two per-area code audits (July 2026), 97 added and re-verified on 27 August 2026, and 233 from the Round-2 build folded in on 29 August 2026 (the seven `Round 2` sections at the end, one per workstream). Of these, **412 deliver analytic or visual substance** and are counted toward the six challenge capabilities; **725 are infrastructure** and are counted nowhere; one is retired and kept for numbering. Every entry is a capability in the deployed app, and every entry carries the tag that decides which of those three buckets it is in.

Every entry ends with a capability tag under the Round-2 counting rules: `[C1 C4]` — delivers analytic or visual substance for those scored capabilities (C1 Advanced Visualization · C2 Criminological Network & Link Analysis · C3 Sociological & AI-Driven Predictive Dashboards · C4 Pattern & Trend Discovery · C5 Network & Behavioural Analysis · C6 AI/ML-Driven Intelligence); `[C6 endpoint]` — a backend-only endpoint, counted and named as such; `[infra]` — infrastructure (search facets, query builders, table chrome, exports, saved views, i18n, loading/empty/error states, shortcuts, theming) that is **excluded from every capability count**; `[retired]` — no longer in the app, kept for numbering. `node scripts/check_capabilities.mjs` derives the scorecard, the overlap matrix and `docs/capability_counts.json` from these tags and fails CI when `CAPABILITIES.md` or `README.md` disagree.
